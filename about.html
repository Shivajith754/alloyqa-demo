Our Story

How Launchpad came to be.

Launchpad was created after experiencing firsthand how traditional product management tools often slow teams down with unnecessary process, bloated settings, and complex workflows.

We set out to build a streamlined workspace designed specifically for ambitious teams who want to keep momentum high, stay focused on priorities, and ship great software faster.
