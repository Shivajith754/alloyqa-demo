What is Launchpad?

Launchpad is a simple workspace for small teams to plan, build, and keep their product work moving forward.

How do we get started?

Start by gathering your next pieces of work, making priorities clear, and inviting your team to build together.

Where can I get help with my account?

Send us a note at support@launchpad.example and include the details of what you’re trying to do.
