Support FAQ

Answers for getting unstuck.

How do I get started with Launchpad?

Start a project, add your next pieces of work, and invite your team from the workspace.

How do I invite teammates?

Open your project settings and use the team invitation option to share access with the people you work with.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. You can move between plans as your team’s needs change. Contact support if you need help choosing the right plan.

Where can I report a problem?

Email support@launchpad.example with a description of the issue and we’ll help you get back on track.
