FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Launchpad?

Launchpad is a simple workspace for small teams to plan, build, and keep their product work moving forward.

Who is Launchpad for?

It is built for fast-moving teams that want clarity and momentum without adding unnecessary process.

How do we get started?

Start by gathering your next pieces of work, making priorities clear, and inviting your team to build together.

Can we use Launchpad as our team grows?

Yes. Launchpad keeps the work visible and gives everyone a clear view of what is happening and what needs attention next.
