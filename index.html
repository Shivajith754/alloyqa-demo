FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Launchpad?

Launchpad is a simple workspace for small teams to plan, build, and keep their product work moving forward.

Who is Launchpad for?

It is made for fast-moving teams that want to stay focused, share context, and turn ideas into clear pieces of work.

How does Launchpad help teams move faster?

By keeping the work that matters visible, Launchpad helps everyone understand what is happening and what needs attention next.

Can we get started quickly?

Yes. Start with the work in front of you, keep the process simple, and build from there as your team grows.
